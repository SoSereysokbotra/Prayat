# ScamSim — Pitch Deck Slide Structure

### Design Blueprint | Inspired by Octo-Fin Top 2 Structure

> **How to read this document:**
>
> - `[IMAGE AREA]` = placeholder where an image or illustration goes. Draw a dashed box in your design tool.
> - `⚠️ DRAFT` = data that needs to be verified or researched before the final pitch
> - **Bold text** = the actual text to put on the slide
> - _Italic text_ = design instruction for you, not text on the slide

> ⚠️ **DRAFT NOTE — IMPORTANT:** This slide needs real numbers. Here is a suggested calculation method:
>
> **TAM (Total Addressable Market)**
> — ASEAN internet users: approximately 450 million (source: DataReportal 2024)
> — If 10% are potential cybersecurity learners paying $3/month = $162 million/year
> — Or: Focus on Cambodia only for credibility. Cambodia internet users: ~10 million (source: Statista)
>
> **SAM (Serviceable Addressable Market)**
> — Cambodian smartphone users who actively use Telegram/Facebook: ~8 million
> — Families with children in school: ~1.2 million families
> — If targeting families + young adults: ~2-3 million potential subscribers
> — At $3/month = $72-108 million/year (SAM)
>
> **SOM (Serviceable Obtainable Market)**
> — Realistically 1-5% capture in year 1-2
> — Target: 5,000-20,000 paying subscribers in year 1
> — At $3/month = $180,000-$720,000/year (SOM)
>
> **Find the right sources:** DataReportal Cambodia 2024, Ministry of Posts and Telecommunications Cambodia, NIDA Cambodia, World Bank Cambodia Digital Economy report.
